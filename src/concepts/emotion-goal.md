# Emotion, Motivation and Enthusiasm

## What it covers in the lectures (paraphrase)

Positive emotion signals progress toward a valued goal and negative emotion signals obstacles or threat. Enthusiasm and motivation follow from having an aim worth pursuing.

## From the lecture

> "Emotion is experienced in relationship to a goal. Positive emotion indicates you're moving towards the goal. Negative emotion indicates that something has gone astray."
