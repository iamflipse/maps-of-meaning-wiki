## What Peterson says (paraphrase)

Near the promised land the Israelites again whine, claim victimhood and pine for tyranny, and God sends poisonous snakes. Instead of removing them, God tells Moses to melt the people's bronze, an inversion of the golden calf made while Aaron, the political wing, gave the people what they wanted, and to raise a bronze serpent on a staff; whoever looks at it is not harmed. Moses' staff is his living authority, a blooming tree of life that becomes a serpent that eats the Egyptian magicians' serpents. The serpent on the staff became the symbol of healing because a small dose of poison fortifies, as in the Greek 'pharmakon', vaccination and learning. The question becomes: what is the worst poison, and what must you look at to be most resilient? The worst is spiritual poison, the poison of hell, like Snow White's apple.

## Key ideas (paraphrase)

- Whining and resentment make a bad situation worse.
- Without a connection to the highest, politics gives people what they want, the golden calf.
- A living tradition blooms; it is tradition plus vision.
- A small dose of what poisons you builds resilience: the essence of healing and learning.
- Ask what you must look at to become most resilient.
