## What Peterson says (paraphrase)

Moses, transformed by the burning bush, speaks truth to Pharaoh: 'Let my people go, so they may worship me in the desert', a vision of ordered freedom under an abstract good. Pharaoh doubles down and the plagues destroy the present and, with the firstborn, the future. You cannot have a tyrant without a slave, in a family or at work, so the Exodus story is always happening; the modern slave is the NPC. The remedy is a vision of your own destiny: Peterson describes having people write out the life they would want in five years, negotiating with calling and conscience until it is thrilling. Pick your load rather than have it thrust on you. The path runs from tyranny through the Red Sea and chaotic desert to the promised land, guided by pillars of light and dark that balance chaos and order. Meaning is a real inner guide, so do not lie and pervert it.

## Key ideas (paraphrase)

- Freedom in Exodus is ordered freedom under a higher good.
- Tyrants who refuse to adjust destroy the present and the future.
- If you live with a tyrant, you are a slave; the story is always happening.
- Write a vision of a life you would be thrilled by, and pursue it.
- The path goes from tyranny through chaos to the promised land.
