# Sacrifice, Work and Service

## What it covers in the lectures (paraphrase)

Sacrificing the present for the future, and the lower for the higher, is the basis of work and of the covenant. Voluntary self-sacrifice in service of the highest is, Peterson argues, the orienting principle of order.
