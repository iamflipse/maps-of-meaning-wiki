# 54. Questions: Moses' Last Mistake, the Holy Fool and the Prayer of Humility

**Part 11: Closing: The Tree of Life and Humility** · transcript words 97,951–98,802 (852 words)

← [53. The Tree of Life and Its Two Fruits](53-tree-of-life.md)

## What Peterson says (paraphrase)

A listener asks why Moses, after warnings against the prideful intellect, did not enter the promised land. Peterson answers that at that stage he was meant to use words, not force, and broke the spirit of the endeavor. As you progress, opportunity and responsibility grow together: from those who have much, much is required. Intellect is a gift to be used in service, not worshiped. In the fairy tale The Water of Life, the arrogant older brother refuses a dwarf's help, while the foolish younger brother admits he doesn't know where to go and is guided and armed. The holy fool is Socrates, wise because he knew his ignorance. Humility is not cringing apology but the opposite of tyrannical pride, the search for a better path: 'Help me find a place I haven't looked' is a prayer that will be answered.

## Key ideas (paraphrase)

- As your field of opportunity grows, so does your responsibility.
- From those who have much, much will be required.
- Intellect is a talent to use in service, not to worship.
- The fool who admits ignorance gets the guidance the arrogant miss.
- Humility is the search for a better path: pray to find a place you haven't looked.
