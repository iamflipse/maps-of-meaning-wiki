Notes on the transcript

  • The transcript is one continuous text with no headings or paragraph breaks. Section and part titles are added by this wiki and follow the changes of topic, including the question-and-answer passages.
  • Quotes keep the transcript's spelling. A few spots in the transcript run words together or mishear names (for example 'pharmacon' for pharmakon, 'Aries' for Ares); quotes containing those were left out where possible, and summaries use the usual spellings.
  • The lectures refer to images shown on screen (paintings, sculptures, diagrams). They are not in the transcript, so summaries describe them only as Peterson does.
