# Truth, Words and Confession

## What it covers in the lectures (paraphrase)

Speaking truthfully is what keeps you and the world from hell. Words bring order out of chaos, confession clears what has been hidden, and truth must be paired with love.

## From the lecture

> "And the Genesis insistence is that that's the pursuit of truth aimed at love."
