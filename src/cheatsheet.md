# Cheat Sheet

The whole lecture series on one page. Quotes are Peterson's, copied exactly from the transcript; everything else is paraphrase.

## The core idea

**Summary (paraphrase):** Peterson argues that the world can be seen as a place of objects or as a forum for action, and that for living, the world of value, aim and story comes first. Enduring myths share a meta-story: order is disrupted by the unknown, the hero voluntarily descends into chaos, faces what is feared, and returns with something that renews the world. Aim upward, listen to conscience, tell the truth, sacrifice voluntarily, and confront what you would rather avoid.

## Main points

### 1. There are two ways of looking at the world

**Paraphrase:** The world as objects and the world as a place to act; the second is primary for how we live.

> "So it turns out that there are two fundamental ways of looking at the world."
>
> [1. Two Ways of Looking at the World: The Frame of Perception](sections/01-two-ways.md)

### 2. Order and chaos

**Paraphrase:** Life is lived on the border between the explored and the unknown.

> "It's a completely different way of looking at the world, to look at the world as a place of chaos and order."
>
> [51. It's Up to You: Facing Evil, and Why Exposure Makes You Braver](sections/51-up-to-you.md)

### 3. Face what you fear voluntarily

**Paraphrase:** Voluntary exposure makes people braver, and the bravery generalizes.

> "They became braver. That's not the same thing."
>
> [51. It's Up to You: Facing Evil, and Why Exposure Makes You Braver](sections/51-up-to-you.md)

### 4. What poisons can cure

**Paraphrase:** The brazen serpent and the pharmakon: look at what bit you.

> "If you look at what poisons you, you become resilient."
>
> [51. It's Up to You: Facing Evil, and Why Exposure Makes You Braver](sections/51-up-to-you.md)

### 5. It's up to you

**Paraphrase:** Responsibility for confronting evil falls on each person.

> "What happens when good people remain silent? Hell dominates."
>
> [51. It's Up to You: Facing Evil, and Why Exposure Makes You Braver](sections/51-up-to-you.md)

### 6. Keep faith in the future

**Paraphrase:** Like Caleb and Joshua, choose life rather than despair.

> "You are required to maintain faith in the future."
>
> [52. Mary, the Scouts of Canaan, and Faith in the Future](sections/52-faith-future.md)

### 7. Humility opens the way

**Paraphrase:** Admitting ignorance is the search for a better path.

> "And the humility is always something like, you could think of humility as the search for a better path forward, right?"
>
> [54. Questions: Moses' Last Mistake, the Holy Fool and the Prayer of Humility](sections/54-holy-fool.md)

## Do this

Practices the lectures themselves call for.

### 1. Face something voluntarily

**Paraphrase:** Pick one thing you are avoiding and approach it on purpose.

> "You can't transform anything without facing it."
>
> [51. It's Up to You: Facing Evil, and Why Exposure Makes You Braver](sections/51-up-to-you.md)

### 2. Master what you confront

**Paraphrase:** Treat each confrontation as a chance to grow.

> "You can master what you confront."
>
> [53. The Tree of Life and Its Two Fruits](sections/53-tree-of-life.md)

### 3. Offer your best

**Paraphrase:** Burn away what is unworthy in what you offer.

> "It means to sacrifice everything unworthy, so that the heavenly city can be established."
>
> [53. The Tree of Life and Its Two Fruits](sections/53-tree-of-life.md)

### 4. Build trust

**Paraphrase:** Be someone others can rely on.

> "High-trust societies are abundant."
>
> [53. The Tree of Life and Its Two Fruits](sections/53-tree-of-life.md)

### 5. Raise children to handle serpents

**Paraphrase:** Foster courage rather than sterile security.

> "It's the fostering of a courageous ethos that can handle all things."
>
> [52. Mary, the Scouts of Canaan, and Faith in the Future](sections/52-faith-future.md)

### 6. Pray to find a place you haven't looked

**Paraphrase:** Treat ignorance as a wellspring of possibility.

> "Help me find a place I haven't looked."
>
> [54. Questions: Moses' Last Mistake, the Holy Fool and the Prayer of Humility](sections/54-holy-fool.md)

## Say it in one minute

**Paraphrase:** See the world as a place to act, not only a collection of objects. Aim upward, listen to your conscience and tell the truth. When chaos breaks in, face it voluntarily: what you confront you can master, and what poisons you can make you stronger. Take on responsibility, keep faith in the future, and stay humble enough to look where you haven't looked.
