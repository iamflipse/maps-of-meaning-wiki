## What Peterson says (paraphrase)

Societies unify by hammering their values into alignment. In Mesopotamia, tribal gods merged into Marduk, with eyes all around his head and the power to speak transformative words: attention and the redeeming word. The Egyptian Horus redeems the willfully blind king by paying attention. Psychic and social unity go together: 'there's no sanity in an insane society'. The Buddha returns from nirvana to bring everyone along, and voluntary self-sacrifice is the redeeming principle. The deepest idea is exhaustive service of the highest: serve the marriage, the children, the community. Peterson describes the Big Five personality traits and notes that self-consciousness is a facet of neuroticism, so his advice to socially anxious clients was to stop focusing on themselves and serve the others in the room.

## Key ideas (paraphrase)

- Societies form by bringing their values into alignment from bottom to top.
- Attention and truthful, transformative speech rule over the other gods.
- There is no individual redemption without redemption of the whole.
- Meaning comes from serving what is highest at every level.
- Self-consciousness is suffering; replace it with service.
