## What Peterson says (paraphrase)

When an anomaly appears, the first response is a disinhibition of emotion and motivation: a flood of possible reactions. A focused game normally inhibits competing ones, which is why athletes can play through injuries; psychedelics and anomaly remove that inhibition. Stress is preparing to do everything at once, which burns future resources, so keeping your life in order builds resilience. Games are nested, so a small sign like lipstick on a collar can collapse a marriage and rewrite the past. The Daoist yin and yang describe the world as order and chaos. Consciousness deals mostly with possibility, because practiced skills like driving become automatic; it is an error detection and correction system.

## Key ideas (paraphrase)

- The first response to anomaly is a flood of possible emotions and actions.
- Stress is preparing to do everything at once, and it ages you.
- Keep things in order so one crisis does not combine with a hundred others.
- Games are nested; a failure at one level can expose a failure far below.
- Order and chaos are what the world is made of; consciousness meets the unknown.
