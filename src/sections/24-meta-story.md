## What Peterson says (paraphrase)

The complete story runs from a stable state through anomaly and collapse to dwelling in the underworld, reconstitution and a new, better game, one that does everything the old one did and more. That is death and rebirth, fall and redemption. To master chaos you master the process of transformation itself, like a shaman, or the seeker who chases the golden snitch, which Peterson links to the alchemical 'round chaos' and to Mercury, praising J.K. Rowling's mythological imagination. Humans are the only creatures that can transform their first principles, which is why Malthusian limits do not hold: we make computers from sand. The real natural resource is trust, which depends on integrity, as with Israel's desert farms and Holland's land reclaimed from the sea.

## Key ideas (paraphrase)

- The meta-story: stability, collapse, underworld, reconstitution, a better game.
- A better game does everything the old one did, plus more.
- Master the process of transformation, not just one domain.
- Humans can change their first principles, so limits are not fixed.
- Trust, built on integrity, is the real natural resource.
