## What Peterson says (paraphrase)

The vierge ouvrante shows the world resting on mother and child, with God inside Mary holding up the crucifix to kings and queens. The son of man, pictured by da Vinci and by Atlas holding up the world, suggests the essence of humanity is responsibility, ultimately for how you face death and malevolence. Christ takes on the sins of the world; to understand atrocity, ask whether you would have enjoyed it. A city where ten people still tell the truth can stand, and you are called to be one of them: you will take risks anyway, so choose them. Abraham's covenant promises that if he aims up and sacrifices, he will be a blessing to himself, gain a deserved reputation and found something lasting. Each new position demands sacrifice; when Abraham agrees to sacrifice Isaac he gets him back: encourage your children in upward sacrifice.

## Key ideas (paraphrase)

- Responsibility, not hedonism or power, is the essence of humanity.
- Recognize in yourself the capacity for every evil.
- Say what you have to say if you are aiming up, and wait.
- Each step up demands a new sacrifice.
- Encourage your children in upward sacrifice and you get them back.
