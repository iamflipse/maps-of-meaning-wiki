# Two Ways of Looking at the World

## What it covers in the lectures (paraphrase)

The opening claim: the world can be seen as a place of objects (what it is made of) or as a forum for action (what to do). Peterson argues the modern West made the first primary and that experience, phenomenology and lived reality belong to the second.
