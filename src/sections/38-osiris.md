# 38. Osiris, Seth, Isis and Horus: How States Decay and Renew

**Part 8: The Hero** · transcript words 71,552–72,647 (1,096 words)

## What Peterson says (paraphrase)

Osiris, god of the state, was once a culture-founding hero but grows old and turns a blind eye to his evil brother Seth (the setting sun, later linked to Satan), as Scar is to Mufasa. Organizations decay when predators, parasites, psychopaths and narcissists move in, camouflaged by confidence that is not competence; the Red Queen warns you must run to stay in place. Seth chops Osiris into pieces, the provinces of Egypt: things fall apart. Isis, queen of the underworld, gathers the pieces, finds the seminal principle and conceives Horus, an outsider alienated from a willfully blind culture, as many young people are.

## Key ideas (paraphrase)

- Great institutions decay through anachronism and willful blindness.
- Psychopaths use confidence as camouflage for incompetence.
- You must keep updating just to stay in place.
- The spirit of a society can be fragmented but not killed.
- Renewal comes from the seminal principle, carried through chaos.
