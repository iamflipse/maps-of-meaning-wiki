# The Meta-Story and the Archetypes

## What it covers in the lectures (paraphrase)

The pattern common to many stories: a stable state, an anomaly, a descent into chaos and a renewal. The archetypal characters (great mother, great father, hero, adversary) are the elements of that meta-story.
