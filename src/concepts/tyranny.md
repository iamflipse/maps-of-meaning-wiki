# Tyranny, Power and Exodus

## What it covers in the lectures (paraphrase)

Tyranny is order gone rigid and exploitative, ruled by power rather than conscience. The Exodus story is the escape from tyranny through the desert toward the promised land, and Peterson warns about psychopaths who exploit.
