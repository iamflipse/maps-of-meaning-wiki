## What Peterson says (paraphrase)

Peterson defends the very old idea that yang is masculine and yin feminine, and explains why the unknown is pictured as feminine: rejection by women is a rebuke to a man's order and also the chance for renewal, as in Groundhog Day. He describes the devouring mother in Sleeping Beauty, the inverted intellect of Lucifer, his three-year-old daughter's dream about her brother no longer being a baby, the mother's task of moving from total care to encouraging independence, the documentary Crumb, and the Grimm Snow White: the evil queen, the hard-working dwarfs, the corset, the comb and the poisoned apple. He argues knowledge is a meta-food, and that Genesis says both man and woman are made in the image of God.

## Key ideas (paraphrase)

- Yin and yang as feminine and masculine is an ancient idea.
- Rejection is chaos for a man's order and also a chance to transform.
- The highest spirits go most wrong when they invert, as with Lucifer and the devouring mother.
- A mother must move from total care to encouraging departure.
- Knowledge is meta-food; ingesting a bad doctrine is like a poisoned apple.
