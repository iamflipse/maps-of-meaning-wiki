# Voluntary Exposure, Stress and Resilience

## What it covers in the lectures (paraphrase)

Facing what you fear voluntarily, in doses you can handle, makes you braver, and that bravery generalizes; accidental exposure makes things worse. This is the basis of exposure therapy and, Peterson argues, of growth.
