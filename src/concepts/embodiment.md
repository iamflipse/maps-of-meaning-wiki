# Embodiment, Imitation and Procedural Wisdom

## What it covers in the lectures (paraphrase)

Knowledge is first acted out, then represented in image and dream, and only later put into words. Children learn by imitating admirable patterns, and apprenticeship passes on wisdom that cannot be fully articulated.
