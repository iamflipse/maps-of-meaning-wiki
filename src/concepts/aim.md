# Aim, the Ideal and the Ladder Upward

## What it covers in the lectures (paraphrase)

You must aim at something, and aim as high as you can conceive. Aims nest in a hierarchy, like Jacob's ladder or the world tree, reaching toward the highest ideal, and that aim shapes what you can see.
